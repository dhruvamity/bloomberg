Playbook from the numbers

Method and caveats
  • Price: Binance USDⓈ-M XAUUSDT perpetual, 1-minute klines from listing (11 Dec 2025) to today. 5m/15m/1h/4h/1D bars are resampled from the same 1m data. It trades 24/7, including weekends.
  • News: every message in the export, timestamps converted from IST to UTC. Headlines within 3 minutes of each other are merged into one event; the event takes the most market-relevant category among its headlines.
  • Anchor: the open of the minute before the headline, because the channel usually posts 10–60 seconds after the release itself.
  • Range vs normal: the high-low range over the window after the headline, divided by the median range of the same window length over the trailing 7 days (20 periods for 1D).
  • Same-hour adjusted: the same range divided by the median range at that exact UTC minute over the previous 20 days. It removes the "US session is always busy" effect.
  • Both stops hit: from the anchor price, would a stop 1× ATR(14) above and another 1× ATR(14) below both be touched within the next 3 bars of that timeframe? That is the case where a long and a short both lose.
  • Shock rate: share of events whose range ran at least 3× normal.
  • Categories come from keyword rules, so some headlines are mislabeled (for example, "Iran" news that is really about oil). Co-occurring headlines are not proof of cause; the lift against random candles is the honest test.
  • The channel is a relay. On the biggest unscheduled moves price often starts moving before the headline appears, which shows up as an elevated "pre-headline" range.
